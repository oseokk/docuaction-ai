# Terms Of Service Draft

This is a working draft for a future DocuAction AI public release. It is not legal advice and must be reviewed before production launch.

## 1. Service Description

DocuAction AI helps users manage documents by extracting key information, presenting AI-assisted analysis results for review, and creating follow-up actions such as reminders.

## 2. User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for:

- Uploading only documents they have the right to process
- Reviewing AI-assisted results before relying on them
- Verifying dates, amounts, obligations, and generated actions
- Keeping account credentials secure
- Using the service in compliance with applicable laws

## 3. AI Result Disclaimer

AI-assisted analysis may be incomplete, inaccurate, delayed, or unavailable.

DocuAction AI does not provide legal, tax, accounting, financial, or professional advice. Generated fields, summaries, and actions are for productivity support only and must be reviewed by the user.

Actions become reliable only after user review and confirmation.

## 4. Document Handling

Users may upload supported document types such as PDFs and images. The service may reject files based on size, MIME type, extension, quality, policy, or security concerns.

The MVP supports logical deletion. A production release must define the physical deletion and retention policy.

## 5. Availability

The service may be interrupted due to maintenance, provider outages, rate limits, infrastructure issues, or security events. Analysis jobs may fail and may require retry or user action.

## 6. Paid Plans

Future paid plans may include:

- Higher analysis limits
- Longer retention
- Export features
- Calendar or notification integrations
- Team or business features

Pricing, refunds, cancellations, taxes, and store payment rules must be finalized before paid launch.

## 7. Prohibited Use

Users must not:

- Upload documents they are not authorized to process
- Attempt to access another user's documents
- Abuse upload, OCR, AI, or storage limits
- Reverse engineer or disrupt the service
- Use the service for unlawful or harmful activity

## 8. Account Deletion

Users should be able to request account deletion before public release. Account deletion should remove or anonymize user data according to the published privacy policy and applicable law.

## 9. Limitation Of Liability

The service is provided as productivity assistance. Users remain responsible for final decisions, filings, payments, deadlines, and obligations derived from documents.

Support contact must be finalized before public release.
